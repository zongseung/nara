# Matching label contract

`src.matching.datasets.import_labels(path, trusted_reviewers=...)` accepts a caller-selected UTF-8 JSONL manifest. Keep the file in protected company storage; the reviewer allowlist is supplied independently, never read from the manifest itself. The importer reads only, rejects duplicate IDs and conflicting pair/evidence labels, and does not activate a release or overwrite decisions.

Each record requires `label_id`, `request_id`, `candidate_id`, `revision_group`, `family_id`, `seller_id`, `purpose`, `category_id`, `target` (true/false/null), `reason`, SHA256 `evidence_hash`, `reviewer`, timezone-aware `reviewed_at`, `trust` (gold/weak), `provenance`, `sampling_origin` (representative/active_learning), and `environment` (production/fixture). A gold record requires human_review provenance and an independently trusted reviewer. Model recommendations cannot be imported as gold. Store raw supporting evidence independently and verify its hash in the company's review process; the importer cannot establish business truth from a claimed hash.

Unknown targets remain unresolved in total counts and distributions; `binary_examples` excludes them instead of turning them into negatives. Weak and active-learning connected components are train-only. Request, revision, product-family, candidate, and evidence-hash links connect components, so changing only sample IDs cannot create an independent holdout. Group definitions must reflect actual product families and revisions.

`freeze_split` targets 50/15/15/20 train/calibration/validation/test by chronologically ordered independent components. Components that span boundaries are entirely `excluded_temporal`, and seller holdouts conflicting with weak/AL components are rejected. Equal-time records can therefore be excluded. Actual ratios can differ and small datasets can lack partitions; inspect assignments, category/purpose counts, unresolved count, and limitations. No performance claim follows from having a split. The content hash freezes all labels, assignments, ratios and seller-holdout configuration before test evaluation; `SplitManifest.write` exclusively creates a new file and refuses overwrite. Retain the referenced label artifact alongside it.

Review selection uses reproducible 50% uncertain/disagreement, 25% novel-pattern, 25% random quotas with explicitly marked random refill when strata are short. Supply the frozen split manifest; absent one, the sampler computes a group/time split itself and selects only train assignments. Selection produces a train-only active-learning batch, not a representative evaluation sample. Random audits of auto-confirmed outcomes are separate: 1% rounded up, maximum 50 per invocation/day. The caller runs once per day and accounts for category omissions. Batch metadata records the seed, selection reasons, shortfall and separate audit IDs. Representative request holdouts must be secured independently before fitting or threshold selection.

No real reviewed business labels or validated category dictionary were supplied for this implementation. Included tests and manual QA use fixture environment only. Bundled signage, signage accessory and generic identity policy packs remain draft; their category names are placeholders requiring business-validated category mappings, requirements, units and tolerances before activation. A policy becoming active is insufficient for production automatic decisions: the release/certificate/source gates still apply.
